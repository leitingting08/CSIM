# Cognitive Science and Psychology: Mind, Brain and Behaviour

## Course Aim

Overview of major topics in Cognitive (Neuro)science.
No prior background in Experimental Psychology is assumed.
Get informed about the field in the context of your own program of research.

## Graphical Abstract

A graphical abstract is NOT a summary, "in spite of what some AI models may say."
Goal: attract interest by highlighting what matters most ("a bit of advertising").
